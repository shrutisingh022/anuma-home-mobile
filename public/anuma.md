# Anuma · AI agents for Indian manufacturers

Every enquiry, from every channel, handled until it's paid. Inside the ERP you already run.

## Who it's for
Businesses that sell in bulk to dealers, distributors, retailers, project buyers and exporters. Your customers order on WhatsApp, email or calls; your team types it all into the ERP.

Industries: fabric and textiles, consumer electronics, electricals, pumps, die casting, pipes and fittings, gears and machining, steel and metals, pharma and chemicals, export and custom goods, handcrafted goods, packaging.

Not for online sellers or marketplace stores yet.

## The agents
- **Inbox**: WhatsApp, email and calls in one shared inbox.
- **Identify**: matches a photo or description to your product code; asks your team when unsure.
- **Quote**: works out the BOM, finds each line in your old quotes, prices it on today's rates.
- **Order**: every message becomes a draft order, saved to your ERP once your team says yes.
- **Distribute**: sends today's stock to every distributor; their orders come back as drafts.
- **Collect**: polite payment reminders on time; matches each payment to its invoice.
- **Remember**: every customer's rate, usual order and habits. Every other agent works from it.

## ERPs
Tally, SAP, Odoo, Zoho, Dynamics 365 and ERPNext. Custom ERPs are connected directly, or start with Excel.

## How we start
1. **Look**: we go through a day of your real orders, quotes and messages.
